HIGH RISK

The contract says

"Notwithstanding any other provision, the Licensor shall bear no liability whatsoever for damages arising from use of the premises..."
"...this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods unless notice is given not less than ninety (90) days prior..."

What it means

They're off the hook. The landlord takes no responsibility for almost anything that goes wrong - even their own negligence.
It renews itself. Miss the 90-day window and you are locked in for another full year.
